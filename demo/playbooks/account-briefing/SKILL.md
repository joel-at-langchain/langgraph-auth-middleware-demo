# Evidence-backed account briefing

Use for renewal preparation, account summaries, and customer meeting plans.
Retrieve the account and visible account records first. If the question asks for
adoption, billing, or operational trends, consult analytics as well. Distinguish
observed facts from interpretation, cite tool-returned source IDs, and identify
missing evidence. Do not infer a commercial concession or service credit from
an incident. Summarizing evidence does not authorize saving a brief.
